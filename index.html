Pukora – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 23 November 2026
Developer / Publisher: Fiction Club
Privacy contact: fictionclub.app@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains how Pukora – Dating & Video Chat handles information when you use the app. It is based on the app information and in-app privacy notice supplied to the developer. The exact operation of platform services and third-party SDKs may change as the app is updated.

1. Information you provide

You may provide profile information, profile pictures, birthday details, images, videos, and information you send through app communication features. The app's in-app notice says that submitted images and videos are sent to the app service server so they can be retrieved and used with app features.

2. Chat, friends and social features

Pukora includes chat, group chat, friends, video-call and call-finder features. Information in profiles and content you choose to share may be visible to other users as part of these features. The in-app notice also describes possible visibility to users of other apps built on the same platform system. Do not share information you do not want other users to see.

3. Camera and microphone

The Android app requests camera and microphone permissions for video-call functionality. These permissions are used when you choose features that need them. Audio/video may be transmitted through the app's service infrastructure to connect participants. The developer does not make a blanket claim here that calls are end-to-end encrypted or never recorded; consult the in-app notices and service behavior for the current implementation.

4. Device, advertising and diagnostics information

The Android build supplied for review contains advertising and measurement SDK components. Depending on which SDKs are enabled in the released version, service providers may process device information, advertising identifiers, diagnostics, crash/performance information, ad interactions or analytics events for advertising, measurement, security and service operation. The exact active SDK list and data practices should be checked against the current release and the providers' disclosures.

5. How information is used

Information may be used to create and display profiles, provide chat and group chat, connect video calls, operate friends and call-finder features, store and retrieve submitted media, maintain security, diagnose technical problems, measure service use and support advertising where enabled.

6. Sharing and service providers

Information may be processed by the platform hosting the app's services and by third-party providers that support enabled functions such as communications, media handling, advertising, analytics and diagnostics. Other users may receive information you choose to place in your profile or share through social features. The specific providers and data flows depend on the production configuration of the app.

7. Data retention and deletion

The in-app notice says users can request deletion of personal data through a link in the profile options. Use that in-app deletion option where available. Data may remain for a period needed to complete deletion, maintain security, resolve disputes or meet legal obligations. Removing the app from your device does not necessarily delete your account or server-stored information. For help with a deletion request, email fictionclub.app@gmail.com from the address associated with your request and include your app username; do not send passwords.

8. Children and prohibited content

Pukora is a dating and social communication service and is not intended for children. Do not use the service if you are under 18. The in-app policy prohibits adult-only sexual content and extreme-violence images. Users should report suspected child exploitation, abuse, or other prohibited content through the app's available reporting tools or the privacy contact above.

9. Security

We use the safeguards provided by the app platform and enabled service providers. No method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Do not submit sensitive information unless it is necessary for the feature you are using.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when app features, service providers or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published at this URL with a revised effective date.
