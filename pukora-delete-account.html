Delete Your Account

You can request deletion of your Pukora account and the personal data associated with it. You do not need to uninstall the app to make a request.

How to request account deletion

  1. Send an email to mail@fictionclub.in.
  2. Use the subject line Pukora Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include the email address or mobile number registered with your Pukora account so that we can identify it.
  4. We may contact you to verify that you own the account before processing the request.
Request account deletion by email

What data will be deleted?

After your request is verified and processed, we will delete your Pukora account and associated personal data held by us, where applicable. This may include your profile information, account details, and user-generated content such as photos, messages, and other content linked to your account.

Data that may be retained

Some limited information may be retained where necessary to meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce our terms, or protect the security of the service. Any retained information will be limited to that purpose and kept only for as long as required.

Request without deleting your account

If you want to ask about deleting specific data while keeping your account, email us at mail@fictionclub.in and describe the data you want removed. We will review the request and explain what can be deleted.

Important: Do not include your password, OTP, or other login credentials in your email. Uninstalling Pukora does not delete your account.
